What is an EOI in a pre-launch project?

An Expression of Interest (EOI) is a token amount you pay to register your interest and get priority in unit allotment before the official launch. Terms differ by builder, so ask for the refund conditions and the allotment process in writing before paying.

Pre-launch or ready-to-move: which is better?

Pre-launch usually means a lower entry price and a staged payment plan, but a longer wait and delivery risk. Ready-to-move costs more but you can use or rent it at once. Check RERA registration and the builder's delivery record either way.

5 checks before booking near an upcoming airport

1) Confirm the RERA registration. 2) Verify infrastructure timelines from official sources, not ads. 3) Review the builder's past deliveries. 4) Get the full cost sheet including taxes. 5) Read the payment plan and cancellation terms.
